## Stratospheric chemistry and ozone

How do chemical reactions, transport, and circulation combine to shape ozone and other trace gases? I study variability in the tropical middle stratosphere using satellite measurements and chemistry-transport model output.

## Causal inference for Earth system science

I use causal discovery and effect estimation to distinguish plausible process pathways from associations, and to quantify how chemical and dynamical factors influence atmospheric composition.

## Model evaluation

Comparing causal pathways in observations and simulations provides a process-oriented way to investigate what models reproduce and where they differ.

## Climate variability and teleconnections

I also study how climate models represent remote connections and circulation patterns across the Earth system, including Arctic–midlatitude teleconnections.
