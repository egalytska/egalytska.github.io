I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Environmental Physics (IUP), University of Bremen. My research focuses on stratospheric trace gases and the chemical–dynamical processes that influence ozone.

I combine satellite observations and chemistry-transport model simulations with causal discovery and causal effect estimation. This helps me examine how processes are connected, quantify their influence, and compare pathways in observations and models.

My wider interests include climate variability, model evaluation, and making causal methods useful for Earth system science.
