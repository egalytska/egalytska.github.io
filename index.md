# Evgenia Galytska

**Postdoctoral researcher · Institute of Environmental Physics, University of Bremen**

I study how chemistry and atmospheric circulation shape ozone variability in the stratosphere, using observations, chemistry–climate models, and causal inference.

My work brings together satellite observations, chemistry-transport model output, and causal methods to investigate the processes behind atmospheric variability and to evaluate how well models represent them.

## Current interests

- Stratospheric ozone and trace-gas chemistry
- Chemical–dynamical interactions
- Causal discovery and causal effect estimation
- Climate model evaluation and variability
